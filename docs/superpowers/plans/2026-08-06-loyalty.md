# Theobroma Loyalty Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Implement the required 5% accrual, 20% redemption and customer-visible bonus history for registered WooCommerce customers.

**Architecture:** Pure calculator and workflow services operate on integer kopecks through a transactional store interface. A WordPress MySQL adapter persists balances and an idempotent ledger; WooCommerce adapters own checkout, order/refund hooks and My Account rendering.

**Tech Stack:** WordPress 7, WooCommerce 10, PHP 8.3, MySQL InnoDB, WooCommerce CRUD/HPOS, plain PHP contract tests, Playwright CLI.

## Global Constraints

- One bonus equals one Russian ruble.
- Accrual is 5% of paid merchandise after discounts, excluding shipping and shipping taxes.
- Redemption is at most 20% of merchandise and never more than the available balance.
- All persisted money is integer kopecks; every ledger mutation is idempotent. Refund reversal may create a negative available balance when already-earned bonuses were spent, and redemption is disabled until it becomes positive again.
- Guest checkout remains available and unchanged.
- The theme only styles the UI; loyalty business logic remains in `theobroma-commerce`.
